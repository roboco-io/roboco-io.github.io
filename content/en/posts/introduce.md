### **Shall We Turn Innovation Into Reality Together?**
"We want to adopt AI and cloud at our company too — but where do we even start?"
Plenty of companies talk about **digital transformation**, yet once it comes time to actually execute, they run into complex technical barriers, in-house infrastructure limits, and gaps in their team's skills. **AI and cloud** keep growing in importance, but without a proper roadmap and real expertise, turning them into results is genuinely hard.

**ROBOCO (Robot Co-worker)** is an **IT consulting firm** founded to solve exactly that problem. Engineers who came out of **global IT companies** such as AWS and Google joined forces to help businesses do more than merely "attempt" digital transformation — to make it **work on the ground** while simultaneously raising **the organization's own capabilities**. ROBOCO's founding members have run a great many cloud and AI projects, and along the way they experienced firsthand that no matter how good a solution you bring in, it won't last unless the company's culture and internal technical strength can support it.

---

## TL;DR

- ROBOCO is an IT consulting firm that helps companies adopt AI and cloud in ways that actually work in practice.
- Our core areas are GenAI-driven development automation, cloud-native platform engineering, digital transformation, and AI transformation.
- We pair immediately deployable solutions with training, so clients internalize the capabilities and keep them after the engagement ends.


#### **"We Achieve Business Innovation Through Technology"**
ROBOCO's mission is simple and clear.

> **To realize our clients' business innovation with the latest AI technology**

To that end, we've defined four primary business areas — **GenAI-driven development automation**, **cloud-native platform engineering**, **digital transformation**, and **AI transformation** — and staffed each with specialists.

- **GenAI-driven development automation** uses AI to automate repetitive coding and testing work, dramatically shortening development time and freeing developers to focus on more creative problem solving.

- **Cloud-native platform engineering** applies modern cloud technologies such as serverless and container orchestration to help companies run scalable, reliable services.

- **Digital transformation** migrates legacy systems to cloud and AI environments and supports the adoption of an agile organizational culture, lifting both operational efficiency and agility.

- **AI transformation** is the process of applying machine learning and AI models across the enterprise to realize data-driven decision making and automation.


---


#### **"You Get Both a Ready-to-Use Solution and the Training to Run It"**
The biggest differentiator ROBOCO emphasizes is that we deliver **"a solution that actually works"** and **"stronger in-house capability"** **at the same time**. Many consulting firms produce excellent volumes of documentation and presentations, but leave the question of how to actually implement any of it on the ground to the client. ROBOCO instead proposes pre-validated **frameworks**, **processes**, and **tools**, puts them to work **immediately**, and runs a **training program** in parallel.

As a result, clients get **clear deliverables** in a **short span of time** while building the foundation for their own people to **understand and operate** the new technologies and processes **on their own**. In other words, because the in-house capability is already there when the engagement ends, the company can **lead** follow-on projects and expansions itself.

---


#### **"Experts From Global IT Companies Are With You"**
Our founding members bring backgrounds including **senior developer and trainer at AWS**, **senior developer at Google**, and **startup front-end and large-scale e-commerce back-end work**. Through countless internal and client projects, they kept encountering the same frustration from companies: "We want to adopt new technology quickly, but finding the right partner turned out to be hard."

That's why ROBOCO calls itself "**Robot Co-worker**" — a colleague who collaborates with robots — expressing our commitment to building the innovation companies want **together** with them. The name conveys that while we make full use of AI and automation, **human capability and collaboration ultimately come first**, and it stands for the philosophy that technology doesn't take work away from people but **helps them focus on more valuable work**.

#### **"Here's How We're Building Digital Innovation"**
ROBOCO has recently been running **digital and AI transformation projects** with a range of companies in Korea and abroad. In one case, we migrated legacy systems to the cloud in phases, **cutting operating costs** while improving **service stability**. In another, we implemented **marketing automation** with generative AI and **improved response rates**. Above all, we hear back that because the client's own team can now operate the newly learned technologies and processes directly after each project, they have a **foundation for long-term self-sufficiency**.
This is what we mean by keeping both keywords — **"execution and internalization"** — front and center: consulting aimed at generating real results for the client.
