**We share what we learn and help teams become self-sufficient.**

Alongside consulting, ROBOCO provides education for developers, non-developers, and students. We openly publish our workshop curriculum, talk materials, and a role definition for embedding AI-driven development in an organization. Training tailored to your company takes place alongside real work at each stage of our [Solutions](/en/solutions/).

## Vibe coding workshop

A vibe coding workshop curriculum designed as two tracks: one for developers and one for non-developers. The developer track targets senior developers, and the non-developer track targets non-engineering roles such as planning and operations.

- [View the curriculum](https://roboco-io.github.io/vibecoding-workshop/)
- GitHub: [roboco-io/vibecoding-workshop](https://github.com/roboco-io/vibecoding-workshop)

## Learning in the age of AI

A 60-minute talk for university students. It covers how to design a learning path by working backward from the outcome you want, top-down learning, and learning through open-source contribution. It also introduces METR data on the length of tasks AI can complete. The talk is published as web slides with speaker notes.

- [View the slides](https://roboco.io/learn-with-ai/)
- GitHub: [roboco-io/learn-with-ai](https://github.com/roboco-io/learn-with-ai)

## Vibe Lead role definition

A Vibe Lead is a change leader who designs, spreads, and improves AI-driven development processes across the organization to achieve the company's business goals. This role description explains why a Vibe Lead is needed and what responsibilities the role carries, and leaders introducing AI-driven development to their organizations can use it when choosing who should take on the role. It is available in Korean, English, and Japanese.

- GitHub: [roboco-io/vibe-lead](https://github.com/roboco-io/vibe-lead/blob/main/README.md)
