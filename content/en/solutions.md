## A software factory your team owns and runs

**Build what you intend, and use what you learn to refine that intent faster.** ROBOCO helps your team establish a production system for continuously building and improving products with AI.

We work with companies bringing outsourced development in-house, businesses pursuing digital or AI transformation, and digital-native teams seeking faster, more accurate delivery beyond vibe coding.

A software factory connects **intent → implementation → verification → deployment → observation and improvement**. Your team sets the goals and criteria for judgment. Together, we define what AI can execute and where human review is needed. Automated guardrails and harnesses apply security and cost criteria throughout this workflow.

- **[Practical pilot](#pilot)**: Validate the workflow through real work, leaving your team with a working result and experience improving it yourselves.
- **[Software factory setup](#factory)**: Build repeatable pipelines, verification environments, operating standards, and the capability to run them.
- **[Operational improvement](#operations)**: Improve the product and its production system while developing your team's ability to evaluate and adopt new technology.

Training and advisory are integrated into real work at every stage. [Certification and compliance consulting](#compliance) remains a specialist service for entering regulated markets.

## 1. Practical pilot {#pilot}

We select a real business task or feature and build a connected development, verification, and deployment workflow. Your team builds a feature and makes the next improvement, learning which systems and capabilities it needs.

### What we do together

- **Clarify the problem and intent**: Define the intended behavior, expected results, and success criteria with your domain experts.
- **Build a working result**: Implement with AI, then verify and deploy against the agreed criteria.
- **Apply guardrails**: Establish security and cost criteria and human review points appropriate to the pilot, and incorporate them into the workflow.
- **Practice improving it yourselves**: Your team observes how the result is used, then iterates on intent and functionality.
- **Define the next stage**: Use the bottlenecks and lessons identified to scope the software factory setup.

**What stays with your team**: A verifiable result, a reusable workflow, practical experience, and a plan for the next stage.

We agree on **scope, timeline, cost, and success criteria for each engagement** during the initial consultation. [Discuss a pilot](/en/contact/)

## 2. Software factory setup {#factory}

We extend the approach validated in the pilot into a production system your team can run continuously. We build an AI-native development process grounded in modern software engineering, with a cloud-native architecture suited to your environment.

### What we do together

- **Make intent and knowledge reusable**: Organize requirements, domain knowledge, and decision rationale so people and AI can use them together.
- **Build development pipelines**: Connect and automate the recurring steps of implementation, verification, and deployment.
- **Design harnesses and guardrails**: Build the environment in which AI work is executed and verified, with security and cost criteria and review and approval procedures.
- **Establish a cloud-native foundation**: Design infrastructure configuration, deployment, and operations so your team can provision and change the environments it needs.
- **Enable domain experts to develop**: Combine practical training and joint development so people without a development background can turn their expertise into features and improvements.
- **Transfer operational capability**: Establish the documentation, guidelines, verification criteria, and skills your team needs to operate and improve the system.

**What stays with your team**: Development environments and knowledge you own, repeatable production processes, and the capability to operate them yourselves.

## 3. Operational improvement {#operations}

Feed lessons from product use into the next iteration of intent, and improve the production system alongside the product. We help even small teams evaluate rapidly evolving AI technology and apply it to their work.

### What we do together

- **Review intent and outcomes**: Check whether features solve the real problem and prioritize the next improvements.
- **Improve verification**: Incorporate discovered errors and recurring problems into tests, guidelines, and review procedures.
- **Manage security and cost**: Review AI usage and cloud operations, then adjust guardrails to the agreed criteria.
- **Evaluate and adopt AI capabilities**: Assess models and tools against real work and incorporate them after verifying their effectiveness.
- **Expand team autonomy**: Use regular reviews and training to broaden the decisions and improvements your team can handle internally.

**What stays with your team**: Operating practices for continuous improvement, accumulated verification criteria, and the capability to respond to technological change.

## **Certification & Compliance Consulting — Entering Regulated Markets** {#compliance}

Entering Korea's regulated markets requires certification. Cloud providers serving the financial sector must pass K-FSI (the Financial Security Institute's CSP Safety Assessment), and companies that need to prove their information security posture must earn ISO 27001 or K-ISMS. ROBOCO accelerates the entire certification journey — criteria analysis, gap review, evidence management, and response drafting — with **AI agents that run inside your private tenancy.**

The materials involved in certification prep are your company's most sensitive documents: security policies, audit evidence, contracts. In ROBOCO's approach, none of it leaves your environment. Because both the agents and the knowledge base operate entirely within infrastructure you own and control, **the process of preparing for compliance never violates compliance itself.**

### Right for you if you're

- A global SaaS/cloud company preparing for the K-FSI assessment to win Korean financial-sector customers
- A company facing initial certification or renewal for ISO 27001 or K-ISMS(-P)
- A company whose headquarters works in English while the audit runs in Korean, making bilingual response and evidence management a burden
- A company where audit know-how accumulates only in individuals — not the organization — forcing a restart from scratch every year

### What's included

- **Criteria structuring and gap review** — We structure the assessment criteria item by item (e.g., K-FSI's 11 control domains, 48 assessment items, and 162 assessment methods) and run gap reviews based on the previous year's findings. Reviews are cross-validated across different LLMs to filter out single-model bias.
- **A regulatory knowledge base (LLM Wiki)** — We compile assessment criteria, applicable laws, internal controls, and evidence into a cross-referenced wiki. Unlike RAG, which re-searches every time, knowledge understood once gets reused — so response speed compounds throughout the audit, and after certification the wiki remains your asset, becoming the starting point for next year's renewal.
- **An evidence management system** — We build a three-layer mapping from assessment item (§X.Y.Z) to control to evidence file, and test accessibility by asking, "When an auditor reviews this item, how many steps does it take to reach which materials?" Auditor feedback is decomposed and classified at the atomic level so nothing slips through.
- **A register of applicable laws** — We collect the full text of the Korean laws cited in the assessment (the Cloud Computing Act, the Regulation on Supervision of Electronic Financial Transactions, the Personal Information Protection Act, and more) and map each to its assessment items, structurally preventing "failure to review applicable laws" findings.
- **Bilingual response management** — We review translation and terminology consistency between English draft responses and the Korean submissions (Korean audits accept only Korean-language documents), resolving the language barrier between headquarters and the Korean audit at the workflow level.
- **Operational internalization** — After certification, we embed guidelines and workflows so your internal team can run evidence collection, verification, and answer-quality control on a repeatable basis.

### Real-world applications

- **K-FSI safety assessment response for a global SaaS company** — We compiled 156 pages of assessment criteria and five applicable laws into a private knowledge base, and cross-validated gap reviews based on the previous year's audit findings across multiple LLMs. We decomposed some 280 auditor feedback items to the atomic level, mapped them to some 50 evidence folders, and ran the daily cycle of feedback processing, evidence submission, and re-request handling as an AI agent workflow — securing both speed and consistency in audit response. Every document was processed exclusively within the client's own environment.

**ROBOCO's role**: Certification preparation partner. The data stays in your tenancy and the know-how stays in your organization; ROBOCO provides the methodology and the agent workflows.
