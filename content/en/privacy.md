ROBOCO (hereinafter "the Company") complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws, and handles the personal information of website visitors and inquirers as described below.

## 1. Personal Information We Collect and How We Collect It

This website is a static site and does not collect personal information directly through account registration or input forms. However, personal information may be processed in the following cases.

- **Email inquiries**: When a user contacts us at contact@roboco.io, we collect the email address and any information contained in the message.
- **Website usage statistics**: This website uses Google Firebase Analytics. Through it, non-identifying usage statistics such as browser and device information, approximate location (country/city level), pages visited, and access timestamps are collected automatically, and cookies or similar identifiers may be used in the process.

## 2. Purpose of Processing

- Receiving and responding to inquiries, and conducting consultations
- Analyzing website usage and improving our services

## 3. Outsourced Processing and Overseas Transfers

- **Google LLC (Firebase Analytics)**: Collection and analysis of website usage statistics. Data may be processed on Google's servers outside Korea. For details, please see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages)**: Website hosting. Access records such as IP addresses may be processed on GitHub's infrastructure when you visit the site.

## 4. Retention and Use Period

- Email inquiry information: Retained for up to one year after the inquiry is resolved, for the purpose of managing consultation history, and then destroyed. If the inquiry leads to a contract, the retention period defined by the laws applicable to that contract will apply.
- Usage statistics: Subject to the default data retention settings of Google Analytics.

## 5. Rights of Data Subjects

Users may at any time request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of the processing of their personal information. Please send requests to contact@roboco.io, and we will handle them without delay. You may also refuse the storage of cookies through your browser settings.

## 6. Our Commitment to Protecting Personal Information

You can find out how the Company handles customer data and what security principles it follows in our [Security Policy](/en/security/).

## 7. Contact

For inquiries regarding the processing of personal information, please contact us at:

- **Email**: contact@roboco.io

This policy takes effect on August 24, 2026. Any changes will be announced on this page.
