ROBOCOHow the software factory works

People set the direction.
AI carries the workflow forward.

An AI-native process grounded in modern software engineering connects intent, release, and improvement. Domain experts and developers work together, with security and cost criteria applied throughout.

What people do

Understand customer problems and set goals and quality criteria. Decide priorities and important changes, and check whether results match the intent.

What AI does

Implement, test, and deploy within defined permissions and verification procedures. Report outcomes and evidence for decisions, and carry feedback into the next task.

  1. 1. Define intent

    Clarify the problem and the expected outcome.

  2. 2. Build with AI

    Use domain knowledge and development standards to create features.

  3. 3. Verify and deploy

    Check quality and release through agreed procedures.

  4. 4. Observe and improve

    Use real outcomes to refine the next intent and features.

Feed what you learn back into intent, and repeat.

Three foundations for continuous delivery

Intent and knowledge

Organize requirements, domain knowledge, and decision rationale as assets people and AI can use together.

Pipelines and harnesses

Connect recurring steps with AI execution and verification environments. Incorporate security, cost, review, and approval criteria.

Cloud-native infrastructure

Design deployment and operations so teams can provision and change the environments they need.

ROBOCO combines experience at global technology companies, AI expertise, and capability transfer. Our goal is a team that understands its production system and can improve it independently.
